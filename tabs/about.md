<p>
Tamir Dresher is a Principal Software Engineer at Microsoft, where he works on Azure Serverless technologies with a focus on Durable Functions, workflow orchestration, and large-scale distributed systems.
</p>
<p>
Throughout his career, Tamir has led development of cloud platforms, developer tooling, and AI-powered systems used by engineers around the world. He is a frequent conference speaker, open-source contributor, and co-creator of innovative developer experiences that help simplify the development of reliable cloud applications. Tamir teaches Software Engineering at the Ruppin Academic Center and is the author of "Rx.NET in Action" (Manning) and "Hands-On Full-Stack Web Development with ASP.NET Core" (Packt).
</p>
